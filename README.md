## THE DIGITAL WORLD NEEDS SECURITY.

Technology moves forward. So do cyber threats.

Businesses increasingly depend on interconnected systems, cloud infrastructure, digital platforms, and sensitive information. Every connection creates opportunities — but also potential vulnerabilities.

Cybersecurity is no longer simply an IT requirement. It is a fundamental part of business continuity, operational resilience, and digital trust.

My objective is to understand how systems work, identify where they can fail, and apply practical security principles to make them more resilient.

## FROM TECHNICAL ANALYSIS TO BUSINESS SECURITY

Cybersecurity consulting connects technical expertise with business needs.

My approach to security assessment is built around four essential stages:

1. **Discover** — Understand the organization's infrastructure, assets, and potential attack surface.
2. **Assess** — Identify vulnerabilities and evaluate their potential business impact.
3. **Prioritize** — Distinguish critical risks from lower-priority findings.
4. **Recommend** — Provide actionable remediation strategies and practical security improvements.

Security should not only identify problems. It should help organizations make better decisions.
