## 01 / THE DOMAIN

Cybersecurity is the discipline of protecting digital infrastructure, applications, networks, and information against unauthorized access, exploitation, disruption, and data compromise.

As organizations increasingly depend on interconnected systems, cloud infrastructure, APIs, and digital services, the attack surface continues to expand.

Security is no longer simply a technical requirement. It is a fundamental part of business continuity, operational resilience, and digital trust.

**The objective is not only to detect attacks, but to understand how vulnerabilities emerge, how they can be exploited, and how they can be prevented.**

## 06 / CYBERSECURITY CONSULTING

Cybersecurity consulting connects technical investigation with practical security improvements.

A professional assessment should identify weaknesses, explain their potential impact, establish their severity, and provide actionable remediation guidance.

**Assessment workflow:**

1. **Scope definition** — identify authorized assets and testing boundaries.
2. **Discovery** — map infrastructure, services, and exposed assets.
3. **Assessment** — examine vulnerabilities and security configurations.
4. **Validation** — verify findings using controlled testing.
5. **Risk analysis** — evaluate likelihood, impact, and business exposure.
6. **Reporting** — document evidence, severity, and technical details.
7. **Remediation** — recommend corrective actions and verify improvements.

## 08 / SECURITY PRINCIPLES

- **Understand before exploiting:** investigate the underlying vulnerability.
- **Evidence over assumptions:** validate findings through reproducible testing.
- **Least privilege:** minimize unnecessary access and permissions.
- **Responsible disclosure:** communicate vulnerabilities through appropriate channels.
- **Defense in depth:** security requires multiple complementary controls.
- **Continuous improvement:** reassess systems as technologies and threats evolve.
